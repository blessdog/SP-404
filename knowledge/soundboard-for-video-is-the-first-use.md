> "It's an awesome sampler that I want to use in my media studio to take clips
> and use as like a soundboard for videos. But I want to explore all the
> possibilities. You can DJ with it, you can use it with this koala app."
> — Ryan, 2026-09-07

**The first deliverable is the clip-to-pad soundboard for video work.** DJ mode,
Koala, and music-making are in scope as exploration, not as the thing that
decides the architecture. Both halves get done; the soundboard is sequenced
first because it is the directive in the ledger.

**How to apply:** any tool proposed for this repo must answer "does this get a
video clip's audio onto a pad, or a pad's audio back into a video, faster?"
before it is built. See [[midi-in-is-notes-only]] for the constraint that
shapes the pipeline.
